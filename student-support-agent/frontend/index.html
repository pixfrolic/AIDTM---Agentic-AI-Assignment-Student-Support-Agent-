Admissions & Criteria

Admission Process & Eligibility

Your clear pathway to joining our esteemed management programs in India.

Eligibility Requirements

Academic prerequisites per degree level

For BBA Programs (3 Years)

Passed 10+2 examination in any stream (Science, Commerce, Arts) with a minimum aggregate of 55% marks from any recognized board.

For MBA Programs (2 Years)

Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 55% marks, plus valid scores in CAT, MAT, XAT, or Lumina Management Aptitude Test (LMAT).

4-Step Admission Process

From application to final enrollment

1
Online Application

Submit academic credentials, entrance test scores, and statement of purpose online.

2
Entrance Assessment

Aptitude and verbal reasoning evaluation test administered online or at campus centers.

3
Technical Interview

Group discussion and personal interview round with management faculty panel.

4
Offer Letter & Enrollment

Final selection notification and fee deposit for seat confirmation.

Admissions & Criteria

Admission Process & Eligibility

Your step-by-step pathway to joining our prestigious engineering programs in India.

Eligibility Requirements

Academic prerequisites per degree level

For B.Tech Programs

Passed 10+2 examination with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Computer Science with a minimum aggregate of 60% marks from a recognized board (CBSE/ICSE/State Boards).

For M.Tech Programs

Holder of a B.E./B.Tech or equivalent degree in relevant branches with a minimum CGPA of 6.5 or equivalent percentage, plus valid GATE score or university entrance test.

4-Step Admission Process

From application to final enrollment

1
Online Application

Submit your academic transcripts, personal information, and preferred course track via the student portal.

2
Entrance Assessment

Appear for the Jackson National Aptitude Test evaluating quantitative and logical reasoning.

3
Technical Interview

One-on-one virtual or campus technical interaction with our senior faculty panelists.

4
Offer Letter & Enrollment

Receive formal admission offer letter and complete admission fee deposit to secure registration.
